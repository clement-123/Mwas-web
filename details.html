2016 Volkswagen GTI

Price: 1,200,000 Ksh

The 2016 Volkswagen GTI is a sporty hatchback that combines performance, style, and practicality. It features a turbocharged engine that delivers an exhilarating driving experience while maintaining fuel efficiency.

Key Features:

  • Engine: 2.0L Turbocharged Inline-4
  • Horsepower: 210 hp
  • Transmission: 6-speed manual or optional DSG automatic
  • Interior: Sport seats, touchscreen infotainment system, Bluetooth connectivity
  • Safety: Multiple airbags, stability control, anti-lock brakes

This GTI is well-maintained and has a spacious interior, making it great for families. It offers a perfect blend of performance and everyday usability.
