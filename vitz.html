Price: 500,000Ksh

The 2012 Toyota Vitz is a compact car that offers excellent fuel efficiency and reliability. It features a modern design, comfortable interior, and a range of practical features.

Key Features:

  • Engine: 1.3L 4-cylinder engine
  • All-Wheel Drive: Standard for enhanced traction
  • Infotainment System: Touchscreen display with navigation and smartphone integration
  • Safety Features: Advanced driver assistance systems including adaptive cruise control and lane-keeping assist
  • Interior: Premium materials, customizable ambient lighting, and spacious seating for five passengers
